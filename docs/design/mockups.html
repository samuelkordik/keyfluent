Keyfluent: key screens

The chosen combination: arcade base (title 20% smaller, no numbers row, stars), stage-select Progress, cracktro Today screen with a Xenon title-case logo, starfield session end. Each frame is the 1000 × 700 minimum window in Tomorrow Night Bright. Animated parts are shown as one frame; with reduced motion they are still.
